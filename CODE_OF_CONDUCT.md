# Code of Conduct

We want HospiLink to be a welcoming project. Be respectful, be constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/Ayaalmadhon2004).

This policy follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
